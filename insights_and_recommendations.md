
## Business Insights

1. **Electronics** delivers strong sales at ~30% profit margin; **Furniture**
   discounts nearly 2x deeper (~18%) for only ~11% profit margin.
2. **Laptops, Cameras, Headphones, Phones** drive almost all revenue --
   the next sub-category doesn't reach half their volume.
3. **Profit growth is lagging sales growth** from 2024 to 2025.
4. **COD** is the clear cancellation outlier.
5. **Consumer segment leads on volume, not efficiency** -- Corporate
   and Home Office show similar margin ratios at smaller scale.
6. **North + South make up ~60% of sales**; East trails at just ~16%.

## Recommendations

1. Cap Furniture discounts at ~10% (in line with Electronics) instead
   of the current ~18%, and reserve deeper cuts for clearance SKUs only.
2. Shift ad spend and stock priority toward Laptops, Cameras,
   Headphones, and Phones since they carry the business.
3. Pull a category-mix and discount-trend comparison for 2024 vs. 2025
   to isolate whether Furniture's growing share is driving the margin gap.
4. Add a small COD handling fee (e.g. ₹20-40) or a 2-3% Card/UPI cashback
   to shift payment mix and cut cancellations.
5. Set a target to grow Corporate + Home Office combined share from
   their current level toward 40%+ of sales over the next 2 quarters.
6. Audit East-region and run a localized promotion to close the gap with North/South.
